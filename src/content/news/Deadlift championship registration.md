Registration for this year's National Deadlift Championship opened today, with organizers confirming both **Open** and **Amateur** divisions across all standard weight classes.

## What's different this year

Qualifying totals for the Amateur division have been lowered by roughly 10% compared to last year, a change organizers say is meant to encourage more first-time competitors to enter. The Open division's qualifying standards remain unchanged.

Weigh-ins will take place the morning of the event, with lifters given three attempts per lift as usual. New this year: a live-streamed leaderboard update after each flight, rather than only at the end of each session.

## Key dates

- **Registration closes**: 6 weeks before event day
- **Qualifying totals due**: Must be submitted with registration, verified via a video submission or a sanctioned meet result
- **Event day**: Weigh-ins at 7 AM, lifting begins at 9 AM

## Should you sign up?

If you've been on the fence about competing, the lowered Amateur qualifying total is worth checking against your current numbers — it may put this year's event within reach even if last year's standard felt out of grasp.

Full qualifying totals and registration are available on the event's official site.
