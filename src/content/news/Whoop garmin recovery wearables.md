Whoop and Garmin both announced new hardware this week, with a shared focus on recovery tracking rather than raw performance metrics.

## Whoop's update

Whoop's new band adds a skin temperature sensor alongside its existing heart rate variability (HRV) tracking, aiming to give earlier warning signs of illness or overtraining. The company says the sensor can detect temperature shifts as small as 0.1°C.

## Garmin's update

Garmin's release focuses on expanding its existing "Training Readiness" score, now factoring in sleep consistency (not just duration) and recent training load. The company also added a new "recovery time" widget that updates in real time after workouts, rather than only refreshing once daily.

## Should you upgrade?

If you're currently tracking recovery manually — resting heart rate, sleep quality, subjective fatigue — either device could meaningfully simplify that process. But if your current wearable already covers HRV and sleep staging, these updates are incremental rather than essential.

Both devices are available for pre-order now, with wider availability expected in the coming weeks.
